# How to Verify Technical Candidates in 2026: AI-Generated CVs, Skills Validation and Hiring Risk

AI-generated CVs and application tools are making it easier for candidates to apply for technical roles. For employers, that means candidate verification is becoming more important.

## What Hiring Teams Should Verify

### 1. Technical skills
Look beyond keywords and assess whether the candidate can demonstrate the skills required for the role.

### 2. Relevant experience
Check whether their previous work matches the complexity, seniority, and responsibilities of the position.

### 3. Evidence of work
GitHub profiles, portfolios, technical projects, and practical assessments can provide additional context.

### 4. Communication and decision-making
Strong technical candidates should be able to explain how they approached problems, why they made certain decisions, and what they learned.
